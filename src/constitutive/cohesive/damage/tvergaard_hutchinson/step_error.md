The error of a step in the response is estimated by the change of the direction of the opening over it, weighted by how much the greatest opening grows.

```math
\tfrac{1}{2}\,\Delta\lambda_\mathrm{max}\,\|\hat{\mathbf{d}}^{n+1} - \hat{\mathbf{d}}^{n}\|, \qquad
\hat{\mathbf{d}} = \frac{1}{\lambda}\left(\frac{\langle\delta_n\rangle}{\delta_n^*},\ \frac{\delta_t}{\delta_t^*}\right)
```

It vanishes when the direction is constant, as for proportional opening, however large the step, and falls as the square of the step otherwise. It vanishes whenever the greatest opening does not grow, as when unloading, and a step that begins at zero opening takes the direction at its end.

The overshoot is how far the greatest opening runs past a kink in the response that it begins before, the largest over the kinks, and falls as the step. The kinks are the openings $`\lambda_1`$, $`\lambda_2`$ and $`1`$, and the greatest opening of a step that begins on the unloading branch and ends past it.

Compression does not open the interface, so it does not enter either. Both are in units of the opening.
