The trapezoidal Tvergaard-Hutchinson cohesive constitutive model.[^1]

The tractions derive from a potential, so the stiffness is symmetric, and the tangential strength follows from the strength and the lengths.
Compression does not open the interface, and has the same secant stiffness as tension, which vanishes once the interface has failed.

**Parameters**
- The strength $`\sigma_\mathrm{max}`$.
- The normal length $`\delta_n^*`$.
- The tangential length $`\delta_t^*`$.
- The opening $`\lambda_1`$ the strength is reached at.
- The opening $`\lambda_2`$ the strength starts to fall at.

**External variables**
- The separation $`\boldsymbol{\Delta}`$ and the normal $`\mathbf{n}`$.

**Internal variables**
- The greatest opening $`\lambda_\mathrm{max}`$.

[^1]: V. Tvergaard and J.W. Hutchinson, [J. Mech. Phys. Solids **40**, 1377 (1992)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-5096(92)90020-3).
