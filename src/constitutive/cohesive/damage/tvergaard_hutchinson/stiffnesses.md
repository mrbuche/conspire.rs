```math
k_{nn} = \frac{\sigma_\mathrm{max}}{\delta_n^*}\left(g + \frac{g'}{\lambda}\frac{\delta_n^2}{\delta_n^{*2}}\right), \qquad
k_{tt} = \frac{\sigma_\mathrm{max}\,\delta_n^*}{\delta_t^{*2}}\left(g + \frac{g'}{\lambda}\frac{\delta_t^2}{\delta_t^{*2}}\right)
```
```math
k_{nt} = \frac{\sigma_\mathrm{max}}{\delta_t^*}\,\frac{g'}{\lambda}\frac{\delta_n\delta_t}{\delta_n^*\delta_t^*} = k_{tn}
```
In compression the term in $`k_{nn}`$ is absent and $`k_{tt}`$ gains $`\tfrac{g''}{2}\,\delta_n^2/\delta_n^{*2}`$, while $`g'`$ and $`g''`$ vanish while unloading.
