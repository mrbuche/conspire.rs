```math
a(\mathbf{F}) = C\int_{S^2}\left[G_a\!\left(w_0\,\mathbf{u}\cdot\bar{\mathbf{B}}^{-1}\cdot\mathbf{u}\right) - G_a(w_0)\right]d\Omega + \frac{\kappa}{2}\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(J^2 - 1\right) - \ln J\right]
```
with
```math
G_a(w) = \int_0^\infty \psi^*(\lambda)\,\lambda^2\,e^{-w\lambda^2}\,d\lambda,
```
$`\psi^*`$ the reduced extensible-freely-jointed-chain single-chain free energy per link, and $`C`$ as in the [Cauchy stress](self::cauchy_stress). The reference term makes $`a(\mathbf{1}) = 0`$.
