```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{T}_{ijkL}(\mathbf{F}) =
\,& \frac{\mu\gamma_0\eta}{J^{5/3}\gamma\eta_0}\left(\delta_{ik}F_{jL} + \delta_{jk}F_{iL} - \frac{2}{3}\,\delta_{ij}F_{kL} - \frac{5}{3}\,B_{ij}'F_{kL}^{-T}\right)
\\
&+ \frac{\mu\gamma_0\eta}{3J^{7/3}N_b\gamma^2\eta_0}\left[\frac{1}{\eta\left(\mathcal{L}'(\eta) + \varkappa^{-1}\right)} - \frac{1}{\gamma}\right]B_{ij}'B_{km}'F_{mL}^{-T} + \frac{\kappa}{2}\left(J + \frac{1}{J}\right)\delta_{ij}F_{kL}^{-T}
\end{aligned}
```
The factor $`1/(\mathcal{L}'(\eta) + \varkappa^{-1}) = d\eta/d\gamma`$ is the only place the reduced extensible chain differs from [Arruda-Boyce](super::ArrudaBoyce), whose $`d\eta/d\gamma = 1/\mathcal{L}'(\eta)`$.
