```math
\mathcal{T}_{ijkL}(\mathbf{F}) = \sum_{n=1}^N\left[\frac{\mu_n}{J}\left(\frac{\partial\mathbf{B}^{\alpha_n/2}}{\partial F_{kL}}\right)_{ij}^{\prime} - \left(\frac{\alpha_n}{3}+1\right)\sigma_{n,ij}F_{kL}^{-T}\right] + \frac{\kappa}{2}\left(J + \frac{1}{J}\right)\delta_{ij}F_{kL}^{-T}
```
